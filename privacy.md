# Lascodia Launcher privacy policy

*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

Lascodia Launcher is a home screen for Android. It works on your device, and keeps what it knows
about you on your device.

## What stays on your device

- **Your apps and how you use them.** The launcher lists your installed apps. With **Usage access**
  (which you turn on), it reads how long you've used each app, to show play time. This never leaves
  your device.
- **Notifications.** With **Notification access** (which you turn on), the launcher shows your
  notifications in its own notification center, lets you reply to them and keeps the last 24 hours
  of cleared notifications so you can find them again. They never leave your device.
- **Contacts.** If you allow it, search also looks through your contacts. Only on your device.
- **Settings, cover art and widgets** you choose are stored by the launcher on your device. A backup
  file is made only when you ask, and saved where you choose. **Send settings** puts the same file
  in Android's share sheet, to send with Quick Share, Bluetooth or a message of your choosing; the
  launcher sends nothing itself. A settings file opened with the launcher is only used if you say
  yes.
- **Play stats.** With Usage access, the launcher keeps its own count of each game's play time per
  day, sessions and longest session (Android keeps only about a week), so a game's stats last longer.
  It stays on your device, isn't in backups, and goes if the launcher is uninstalled.
- **Retro game folders.** If you add RetroArch's folder or a folder of games, the launcher reads
  the file names, RetroArch's playlists and box art in those folders only (Android asks you to allow
  each folder), to show the games in the Library. It reads nothing else on your storage, and a game
  opens in RetroArch or the app you chose for that folder.

- **Calendar.** If you allow it, the Today card on Home shows your next events. Read on your device
  only.
- **The console overlay.** If you turn it on (it's an Android accessibility service, off until you
  agree to it in the launcher and switch it on in Android's settings), it sees which app is open, to
  apply app lock, kids mode, bedtime and daily limits; and it reacts to a game controller's guide,
  Select, Start and B buttons and the volume keys, to open the guide menu and the volume pop-up.
  Every other key and anything you type passes straight through unread. It doesn't read what's on
  the screen. It takes a screenshot when you choose Screenshot in the guide menu, saved to
  Pictures › Lascodia on your device; and, while a game is open, a picture of it every few minutes
  and when you press the menu button, to show on the game's card on Home ("Where you left off"). Those pictures stay in the launcher's
  own storage on your device, aren't included in backups, and are deleted if you turn the feature
  off. It also shows break reminders over a game if you turn them on, and the battery's temperature
  and how fast it's going down while a game is open (read from Android, not kept). For apps you choose to leave
  their own controls to (such as a PC-game app), it stops listening to keys while they're open.
  Nothing it sees leaves your device.
- **Shizuku or root.** If you set either up, the launcher can close apps completely and check which
  apps are running, to offer Close in an app's options and to confirm an app really closed. That
  list is read on your device only and isn't kept.
- **Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.** If you allow it, the launcher lists Wi-Fi networks and Bluetooth devices
  nearby so you can connect controllers and headphones without leaving it. On Android 13 and newer
  this uses "Nearby devices" permissions that don't give location; on older versions Android
  requires the location permission to list them. The launcher never uses or keeps your location.
- **Family features.** Parental PIN (stored only as a salted hash), kids mode, bedtime, daily limits,
  guest mode and the weekly report all stay on your device.

## What goes over the internet

- **Weather.** If you choose a town for the Today card, the launcher asks Open-Meteo
  (open-meteo.com) for its weather every half hour, sending only that town's coordinates. Searching
  for a town sends what you typed to Open-Meteo's place search. Your location is never used.
- **Theme fonts.** Each theme uses its own typeface. If your device doesn't have it, the launcher
  downloads it once from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), sending only the
  font's name, and keeps it on the device. Google receives what any web request carries (such as your
  IP address); see Google's privacy policy.
- **The theme gallery.** Opening it downloads the list of themes from GitHub
  (raw.githubusercontent.com). No information about you is sent, beyond what any web request
  carries (such as your IP address). On copies not installed from Google Play, **Check now** in
  Settings downloads the same list to read the newest version number, only when you tap it.
- **Paid themes.** Purchases are made through Google Play. When you buy a paid theme, the launcher
  sends the purchase receipt (a token from Google Play, the product's name and the app's name) to
  the Lascodia theme server, which checks it with Google and sends back the theme. The server does
  not keep the receipt or anything else about you.

Lascodia Launcher has no ads, no analytics and no account.

## Your choices

Usage access, Notification access, the console overlay, contacts, calendar, nearby devices and
"Modify system settings" are all optional and can be turned off in Android's settings at any time;
the launcher keeps working without them.
